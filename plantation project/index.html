About Our Project

This 8-week Community Service Project on Plantation was conducted from 01/06/2026 to 26/07/2026 across two schools in Padhanavthi Centre, Podhalakur Road, Nellore District.

Fieldwork Phase (01/06/2026 to 12/07/2026): Planting saplings, conducting environmental awareness sessions for students, and distributing plants to encourage a greener community.

Mini Project Phase (13/07/2026 to 26/07/2026): Designing and developing the Green Village Plantation Web Application and compiling the final project report.

Project Objectives

  • Increase green cover around local school campuses
  • Educate school students on tree care and environmental conservation
  • Distribute saplings to encourage home and community gardening
  • Build an informative web portal documenting the plantation drive
